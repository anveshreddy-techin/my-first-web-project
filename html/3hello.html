1. THE BEGINING

The beginning of Naruto introduces the titular character, Naruto Uzumaki, as a young ninja in training, ostracized by the villagers of Konohagakure due to the Nine-Tailed Fox sealed within him. He strives to become Hokage, the village's leader, to gain recognition and respect. The initial episodes focus on Naruto's graduation from the Ninja Academy, his formation of Team 7 with Sasuke and Sakura, and their first challenging missions. These early episodes establish the core themes of the series: Naruto's ambition, the importance of teamwork and friendship, and the struggles of a young ninja in a world of hidden dangers
