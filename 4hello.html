Naruto[a] is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Masashi Kishimoto. It tells the story of Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who seeks recognition from his peers and dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his village. The story is told in two parts: the first is set in Naruto's pre-teen years (volumes 1–27), and the second in his teens (volumes 28–72). 1. THE BEGINING

The beginning of Naruto introduces the titular character, Naruto Uzumaki, as a young ninja in training, ostracized by the villagers of Konohagakure due to the Nine-Tailed Fox sealed within him. He strives to become Hokage, the village's leader, to gain recognition and respect. The initial episodes focus on Naruto's graduation from the Ninja Academy, his formation of Team 7 with Sasuke and Sakura, and their first challenging missions. These early episodes establish the core themes of the series: Naruto's ambition, the importance of teamwork and friendship, and the struggles of a young ninja in a world of hidden dangers


2.THE JOURNEY


Naruto Uzumaki's journey is a story of overcoming adversity and achieving one's dreams. From being an ostracized orphan, he transforms into a powerful ninja and the Seventh Hokage of his village. His journey is marked by his unwavering determination to gain the village's acknowledgement, his dedication to his friends, and his commitment to protecting his loved ones.
